All night long

What is happening on the floor

Pep rally

Meet every player as they run out to their walk up song, with coach hype and team chants.

Three point showdown

Boys vs girls. Four shooters a side, five racks of five, then a sudden death shot for the ultimate winner.

Crowd games

Minute to win it games, a grade vs grade relay and halftime shots with prizes.

Concessions

Pizza, nachos, fries, boiled peanuts, drinks and the Bronco Meal Deal. All money raised supports the program.
