1Methodology and index calculation formula

A weighted composite of per game averages. The index rewards high impact actions, with extra weight on steals, blocks and assists, which drive transition offense and defensive success in high school basketball.

Stat categoryWeightTactical impact
Points per game (PPG)× 1.0Direct offensive output and scoring capability.
Rebounds per game (RPG)× 1.2Possession control, finishing defensive stops and second chance creation.
Assists per game (APG)× 1.5Pass first efficiency, court vision and creating shots for teammates.
Steals per game (SPG)× 2.0Defensive disruption, forcing turnovers and igniting fast breaks.
Blocks per game (BPG)× 2.0Rim protection, interior deterrence and shot contests.
Composite index formulaIndex = PPG + (RPG × 1.2) + (APG × 1.5) + (SPG × 2.0) + (BPG × 2.0)
