Privacy Policy

The Middleburg High School Lady Broncos girls basketball program runs mhsladybroncos.com to share our schedule, players and highlights and to raise money for the team. Many of the people on this site are high school students, so we collect as little as we can and we treat their information with care.

Our student athletes

We show a player's name, jersey number, position, class year, height, basketball stats, photos and highlight videos only to celebrate the team and help her be seen by college programs. A parent or guardian gives permission before a player's fundraiser page or photo goes live, and a coach approves photos before they are shown.

We never post a player's home address, phone number, personal email, school schedule or grades on this site. College coaches and media who want to reach a player are asked to contact the head coach, and contact details are shared only with the family's permission.

A parent, guardian or player who is 18 or older can ask us at any time to change or remove any photo, video, stat or detail about her. We act on these requests as quickly as we can, usually within a few days.

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Donation forms. Your name, email address, the amount you plan to give and who you are supporting. You can choose to appear as Anonymous. Payments are made on MySchoolBucks, the official Middleburg High School payment portal. We never see or store card or bank details.
  • Sponsor forms. Your business name, contact name, email, phone, website, social media accounts, logo and the details you give us so we can deliver your sponsor benefits.
  • Player accounts. A player's name, jersey number, position, photo, personal message and the guardian permission record. Players can add a list of family and friends (names with an email or phone number) who may want to support the team.
  • Supporter emails. If a player adds you as a supporter, we keep your name and the email or phone number she entered so the team can send a small number of fundraiser updates on her behalf.
  • Basic visit information. Our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, which help keep the site secure. We also count page visits, shares and donation button taps without tying them to your name.

How we use it

  • To run the fundraiser, confirm gifts with the school and thank our supporters.
  • To deliver sponsor benefits and send each sponsor a report of what we delivered.
  • To send fundraiser emails that a coach has approved. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and we stop right away when you use it.
  • To show the public parts of the site: the schedule, highlights, player cards, sponsors and supporter thank yous. Public supporter names show as a first name and last initial, or as Anonymous.
  • To keep the site secure and working well.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with the services that help us run the site, and only what each one needs:

  • Netlify hosts the site and stores our records.
  • Resend delivers approved fundraiser emails.
  • MySchoolBucks and Middleburg High School process and record donations and sponsorships.
  • YouTube and Instagram show our highlight videos and posts. When you play a video or view a post, those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. Videos load in YouTube's privacy enhanced mode until you press play.
  • Elevate Her Hoops Report hosts our players' recruiting profiles, and this site shows the photos and stats posted there.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this site.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our players, families and community.

Cookies and storage

We use a few small cookies that are needed for the site to work: one keeps players and coaches signed in, and one remembers that you entered the team code for the playbook. Your browser also briefly remembers which pages you have visited so we do not count the same visit twice, and saves a player's supporter list as she types it. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from fundraiser emails with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete information about you or your child.
  • Give anonymously, so your name is never shown on the site.
  • Ask a sponsor listing, logo or supporter thank you to be removed.

How we protect information

The site uses encrypted connections. Coach and player areas need a password or login code, logins are limited to stop guessing, and private pages are hidden from search engines. Only the coaching staff can see donor, sponsor and supporter details. We keep fundraiser and sponsor records as long as the school needs them for its records and delete supporter lists when they are no longer needed.

Contact us

Questions, removal requests or concerns go to Head Coach Tenise Haynes at tenise.haynes@myoneclay.net. We will update this policy when our practices change and show the new date at the top of this page.
