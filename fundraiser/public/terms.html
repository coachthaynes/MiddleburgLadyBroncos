About this site

This site is run by the Lady Broncos girls basketball coaching staff at Middleburg High School to share team news and support the program. Schedules, scores, stats and other details can change, and while we work to keep them accurate, please check with the team for the latest information.

Respect for our athletes

Our players are high school students. You may not copy, save, repost or use any player's name, photo, video or information for any commercial purpose, or in any way that harasses, embarrasses or endangers her. You may not use anything on this site to locate, contact or follow a player outside of official channels. College coaches, recruiters and media should contact the head coach.

Recruiting contact must follow the rules of the NCAA, NAIA, NJCAA and the Florida High School Athletic Association, as they apply to you.

Donations

Gifts are made to Middleburg High School through MySchoolBucks, the school's official payment portal, and are used for the Lady Broncos basketball program. The donation form on this site records your pledge so we can thank you, and a coach confirms it against the school's report. Gifts are generally not refundable once received by the school. Questions about a payment, receipt or tax treatment should go to the school or to your tax adviser.

Sponsors

Sponsor levels, prices and benefits are listed on the sponsor page and in the sponsor packet. A sponsorship is confirmed once a coach accepts it and payment is received by the school. Benefits are delivered during the season they are purchased for, and some depend on the game schedule and school rules. By sending us a logo or business details you confirm you have the right to share them and allow us to show them on the site, banners, shirts and team social media for the sponsorship.
