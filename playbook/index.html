Dark circles are the offense: players 1 through 5. Red X marks are the defense: defensive players 1 through 5. When our defense runs man, defensive player 1 guards player 1, defensive player 2 guards player 2, and so on.

The lines

Cut: a player runs without the ball Dribble: a player moves with the ball Pass: the ball moves between players Screen: a player sets a pick Defensive movement The ball

On any play: Space plays or pauses, the right arrow goes forward one step, and the left arrow goes back.
